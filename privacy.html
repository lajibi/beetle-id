隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 5 月 21 日

1. 概述

甲虫识别（"本应用"）由灵旭游戏（深圳）有限公司（"我们"）开发和运营。本应用以隐私为核心设计原则，所有识别和数据处理均在您的设备上本地完成。

2. 我们不收集的数据

  • 您拍摄或选择的照片
  • 识别结果和物种信息
  • 饲养记录、繁殖记录、喂食提醒等个人数据
  • 您的位置信息（仅在设备端用于标记观察记录，不上传）
  • 浏览历史或使用行为分析数据

3. 我们收集的数据

如果您订阅 Pro 版本，Apple 的 App Store 支付系统会处理您的付款信息。我们通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 接口验证您的订阅状态，不会接触您的支付卡详情。

我们不运营任何接收、处理或存储您个人数据的服务器。

4. 设备权限

  • 相机：用于拍摄甲虫照片进行识别，照片不会上传。
  • 照片库：用于从相册选择照片进行识别，以及保存观察记录照片。
  • 位置：可选，用于在观察记录中标记发现地点，位置数据仅存储在设备上。
  • 通知：可选，用于喂食提醒功能。

5. 数据存储

所有数据（饲养记录、繁殖记录、观察记录、喂食提醒、成就等）均使用 Apple 的 SwiftData 框架存储在您的设备上。删除应用将删除所有数据。

6. 第三方服务

本应用使用 Apple 的翻译框架提供物种描述翻译功能，该翻译在设备上完成。除 Apple 的 App Store 支付系统外，本应用不集成任何第三方 SDK、广告或分析服务。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 您的权利

您可以随时通过 king1856601@gmail.com 联系我们，请求访问、更正或删除您的个人数据。

9. 变更

我们可能会不时更新本政策。变更将在本页面发布，并更新生效日期。

10. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请联系 king1856601@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

1. Overview

Beetle Identifier ("the App") is developed and operated by Lingxu Games (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", "our"). The App is designed with privacy as a core principle. All identification and data processing is performed locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Photos you capture or select
  • Identification results and species information
  • Breeding logs, breeding records, feeding reminders, and other personal data
  • Your location information (used only on-device to tag observation records, never uploaded)
  • Browsing history or usage analytics

3. Data We Collect

If you subscribe to the Pro plan, Apple's App Store payment system processes your payment information. We verify your subscription status through Apple's StoreKit API and do not access your payment card details.

We do not operate any servers that receive, process, or store your personal data.

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used to photograph beetles for identification. Photos are not uploaded.
  • Photo Library: Used to select photos for identification and save observation photos.
  • Location: Optional, used to tag observation records with discovery locations. Location data is stored only on your device.
  • Notifications: Optional, used for feeding reminder functionality.

5. Data Storage

All data (breeding logs, breeding records, observation records, feeding reminders, achievements, etc.) is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Deleting the App will delete all data.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple's Translation framework to provide species description translation, which is processed on-device. Other than Apple's App Store payment system, the App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising, or analytics services.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at king1856601@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at king1856601@gmail.com.
